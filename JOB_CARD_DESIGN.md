## 📱 Responsive Design

### **Desktop (> 1024px)**
- 3-4 cards per row
- Full spacing and padding
- All animations enabled

### **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**
- 2-3 cards per row
- Slightly reduced spacing
- Maintains animations

### **Mobile (< 768px)**
- 1 card per row
- Compact padding (20px)
- Logo: 48x48px
- Location/salary stack vertically
- Full-width button
